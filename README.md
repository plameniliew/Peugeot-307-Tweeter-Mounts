# Peugeot-307-Tweeter-Mounts
Custom 20-degree tweeter mounts for Peugeot 307 Facelift, designed in Fusion 360 and validated with 3D printing.
This is my custom-designed tweeter housing project for the **Peugeot 307 Facelift**, developed as part of an upgrade to my car's audio system.

The factory tweeter mounts were not suitable for my **Hertz Mille Pro MPK 165.3** speaker system, so I designed and 3D-printed replacement housings using Autodesk Fusion 360.

### Design Features
- Custom-fit mounting base designed for the original dashboard locations.
- **20° angled housings** to direct the tweeters toward the center of the cabin.
- Left and right versions with mirrored geometry.
- Hollow construction to reduce material usage.
- Integrated retaining clips for installation in the factory mounting locations.
- Designed to accommodate Hertz Mille Pro tweeters.

### Printing and Installation
The final adapters were printed in **ABS** and successfully test-fitted in my Peugeot 307 Facelift.

A small amount of silicone or super glue may be required to secure the tweeters inside the housings, depending on printing tolerances.

**Important:** Please check the print orientation and support placement before printing. Two retaining clips on my left adapter had minor printing defects, although these did not affect the final fitment.

### Files Included
The uploaded STL contains both left and right tweeter housings.

This project is shared to help other Peugeot 307 owners upgrade their audio systems without permanently modifying the dashboard.
